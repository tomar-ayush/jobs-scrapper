    Email recipients

    Who gets the digest. If empty, the NOTIFY_EMAIL secret is used.

    Company job boards

    The slug is the company part of the board URL, e.g. boards.greenhouse.io/airbnb, jobs.lever.co/palantir, jobs.ashbyhq.com/openai. For Workday use nvidia.wd5/NVIDIAExternalCareerSite from nvidia.wd5.myworkdayjobs.com/NVIDIAExternalCareerSite. Checked before saving.

    Search queries

    Used for LinkedIn and Indeed. If empty, the defaults in config.py are used. Two or three broad queries beat many overlapping ones.

    Company blocklist

    Scam or spam companies. Their jobs are dropped before scoring, never emailed, and any already stored are deleted. Legal endings like Pvt Ltd are ignored when matching.

    Pipeline settings

    These override config.py on the next run without redeploying. Reset goes back to the default.
